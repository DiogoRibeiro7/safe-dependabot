# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to keep project discussions respectful, technical, and focused on the work.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, or deliberately disruptive behavior are not.

## Scope

This standard applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces associated with Safe Dependabot.

## Enforcement

Behavior that violates this standard may be removed or moderated by the repository maintainer. Serious or repeated violations may result in restrictions on participation.

For sensitive reports concerning contributor conduct, contact the repository maintainer privately rather than opening a public issue.
